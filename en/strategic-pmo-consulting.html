FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How Strategic PMO Differs from a Traditional PMO

Strategic PMO connects projects with strategic priorities, portfolio decisions, resource allocation, and benefits realization, extending its role beyond status tracking.

When should an organization have a Strategic PMO?

When several major initiatives run at the same time, dependencies are significant, or executives need information to make portfolio and investment decisions.

Can Elite Knight conduct a Project Health Check?

Yes. We assess scope, timelines, budgets, risks, issues, dependencies, governance, and readiness to recommend corrective actions.
