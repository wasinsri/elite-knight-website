Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data

This page explains how Elite Knight Co., Ltd. collects, uses, and protects the personal data of people who contact us or use this website.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Data controller

Elite Knight Co., Ltd., 1/128 Imm Amphon Village, Soi Thawi Watthana 20, Thawi Watthana Road, Sala Thammasop, Thawi Watthana, Bangkok 10170, is the controller of personal data collected through www.ek.co.th and the company's contact channels.

Data we collect

  • Details you give us when you get in touch: name, email address, phone number, company or organization, topic of interest, and whatever you write in the contact form, an email, or tell us by phone.
  • Website usage data: if you accept cookies, Google Analytics records information such as pages viewed, time on site, device and browser type, and approximate location, which we see as aggregate statistics.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider and the third-party services the website loads receive your IP address as part of normal internet operation.

We do not intend to collect sensitive personal data. Please do not send it through the form or by email.

Purposes and legal bases

  • To answer your enquiry, provide service information, and prepare proposals you ask for, based on your consent and on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To analyze usage and improve the website, based on the consent you give in the cookie notice.
  • To comply with the law and protect the company's rights, based on legal obligation and legitimate interest.

We do not use details sent through the contact form for marketing messages unless you give separate consent.

Sharing and third-party providers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as needed with providers that make the website and our communication work:

  • Our email provider, to receive and answer your messages.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website.
  • Google, for Google Analytics (with your consent), Google Fonts, and the Google Maps embed on the Contact page.
  • Unsplash, which serves images shown on the website.

Some providers may store or process data outside Thailand under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose data when required by law or by an order of a competent authority.

Retention

We keep contact details for as long as needed to answer your enquiry and follow up on related work. If we enter into a contract, we keep data for the period required by law or by that contract. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), you have the right to:

  • Access your data and obtain a copy.
  • Have inaccurate or outdated data corrected.
  • Have data deleted, destroyed, or anonymized.
  • Restrict or object to the collection, use, or disclosure of your data.
  • Receive your data or have it transferred to another controller.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee.

Send requests to info@ek.co.th. We will respond within the period set by law and may ask for information to verify your identity.

Data security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We limit access to contact details to people who need them to answer your enquiry, and we apply appropriate technical and organizational measures against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Cookies

The website remembers your language and cookie choices in your browser and uses analytics cookies only with your consent. Read the details and change your choice in our Cookie Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The latest version is published on this page with its revision date.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact info@ek.co.th or call 063-664-1555 during office hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM.
